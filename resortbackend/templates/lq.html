Dreamland Resort offers spacious and comfortable accommodation amidst a natural landscape. The resort welcomes guests with beautifully appointed guestrooms, the 4 different room types are designed with the needs of different travellers in mind. Each room comes with an attached sit out with a view to the gardens or pool so guests can enjoy the lush, natural surroundings.


Rooms and suites

Pool Suite
Our Signature room offering a private outdoor space and a 100sqft plunge pool.The pool suite is sure to leave you spell bound!

Family Rooms
The spacious rooms comprise of two large bedrooms with 2 attached toilets - ideal for a family or a group of friends between 4-6pax.

Executive Rooms
Well appointed rooms overlooking a majestic fig tree, ideal for travellers on a budget or ones looking for a quick getaway from the city!
